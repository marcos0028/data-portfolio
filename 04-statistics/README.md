# Social Study: University Students in Spain and Italy

## Overview

This project explores differences in university students' perceptions of their future working lives in Spain and Italy. Using survey data collected from students at the University of Valladolid (Spain) and the University of Bologna (Italy), the study applies statistical analysis to compare their perspectives and identify potential similarities and differences.

The project combines data preparation, exploratory analysis and statistical comparison to transform survey responses into meaningful insights.

## Objectives

- Compare students' perceptions of their future careers across two European university contexts.
- Explore similarities and differences between students in Valladolid and Bologna.
- Prepare and clean survey data for statistical analysis.
- Apply statistical methods to investigate patterns in students' responses.
- Interpret and communicate findings in a clear, structured way.

## Dataset

The study is based on a survey of **229 university students**:

| University | Country | Number of students |
|---|---|---:|
| University of Valladolid | Spain | 119 |
| University of Bologna | Italy | 110 |
| **Total** | | **229** |

The dataset contains students' responses concerning their perceptions of the future labour market and working life.

## Methodology

The project follows a structured statistical analysis workflow:

1. **Data preparation:** Review, clean and organize survey responses for analysis.
2. **Exploratory data analysis:** Examine response distributions and summarize relevant variables.
3. **Statistical comparison:** Compare responses between the Spanish and Italian student groups.
4. **Interpretation:** Identify relevant patterns and differences in the data.
5. **Communication:** Present the results in the context of the study's research objectives.

## Tools and Technologies

- **R:** Statistical analysis and data exploration.
- **MATLAB:** Data processing and analysis.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Statistical analysis and comparison
- Survey data analysis
- Interpretation of quantitative results
- Data-driven research and communication

## Project Context

This project was developed as an academic study involving students from Spain and Italy. It demonstrates how statistical techniques can be used to investigate social questions and compare perspectives across different university contexts.

The results should be interpreted in light of the sample size and the characteristics of the participating students, rather than assumed to represent all university students in either country.
